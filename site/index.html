A quick note for this little iPhone.

This repo targets a jailbroken iPhone 6 on iOS 12.5.8. Use rootful iphoneos-arm packages containing arm64 binaries. Sileo manages packages; it doesn’t jailbreak your phone. Check the dependencies and test each tweak on your phone before publishing.

Adding the source manually

In Sileo, open Sources → + and paste https://realphoni.github.io/phoni-repo/. If the Add to Sileo button doesn’t open the app, use this method.

Why are there no packages yet?

This is a fresh home for Phoni’s own packages. Once a built .deb is added to debs/ and the workflow succeeds, it appears here and in Sileo. Starter source files are not installable packages.
