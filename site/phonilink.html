PHONILINK / FIRST BETA

a little call,
over Wi-Fi :)

PhoniLink lets my old iPhone call my newer ones nearby. No SIM, account, or internet server needed.

Download companion IPA ↓ iPhone 6 / Sileo package ↗

on your other iPhone

Download the IPA on your computer and re-sign/install it with your sideloading tool. Sideloadly ↗ supports Windows and phones without a jailbreak. Tapping this download in Safari won’t install the app.

On the jailbroken iPhone 6, install PhoniLink from Sileo instead.

then, on both phones

  1. Join the same Wi-Fi and open PhoniLink.
  2. Give each phone a name and turn on nearby calling.
  3. Allow Microphone and Local Network when asked.
  4. Check the other phone’s name, tap it, and press Call. Press Answer there.

Keep both apps open and unlocked. This beta ends the call when either app goes into the background. There’s mute, speakerphone, decline, and hang up in the app.

This is a separate calling app; it doesn’t reroute regular Phone calls. Apple’s nearby connection can also use peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. Try the earpieces or headphones first if both phones are close together.

Version 0.1.0 beta 1. Compiled and package-checked, still needs testing on real phones. The companion targets iOS 12 and later; your specific newer iOS version hasn’t been verified.
