# Asset provenance

The cube in `site/assets/mark.svg` is reused from the inline logo in
[realphoni/home/index.html](https://github.com/realphoni/home/blob/main/index.html),
at the owner's request. `CydiaIcon.png` is a raster rendering of that cube.

The mint palette (`#46ffb4`, `#eaf3ee`, `#9db3aa`) and the translucent gradient
treatment are adapted from [home/style.css](https://github.com/realphoni/home/blob/main/style.css).
The layout, CSS phone illustration, and package catalog are new.

No third-party tweak binaries or screenshots are bundled. System fonts keep the
site self-contained and avoid loading extra font resources on the iPhone 6.
